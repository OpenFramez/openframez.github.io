# Pixelary - Privacy Policy Supplement

## Privacy-First Architecture

Pixelary is designed with a privacy-first approach. We collect ZERO user data.

## What We Don't Collect

- No analytics (no Google Analytics, no Matomo, no Plausible, no Fathom)
- No tracking pixels
- No advertising cookies
- No login/registration
- No IP logging on our servers (we have no servers — GitHub Pages may keep operational logs but we don't have access to them)
- No browser fingerprinting
- No behavioral tracking

## What We Store Locally (in your browser)

- `pixelary-theme`: Your theme preference (light/dark) in localStorage
- Service Worker cache: Static files for offline use
- HTTP cache: Standard browser caching

This data never leaves your device. Clearing your browser data removes it completely.

## Third-Party Resources

Pixelary loads the following third-party resources:

1. **Wikimedia CDN** (`upload.wikimedia.org`): Photo thumbnails
   - We display images hosted by Wikimedia Commons
   - Wikimedia may log requests per their [privacy policy](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy)

2. **Google Fonts** (`fonts.googleapis.com`, `fonts.gstatic.com`): Vazirmatn font
   - Google may log font requests per their [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

If this concerns you, use browser extensions like uBlock Origin or Privacy Badger.

## Future Plans

In Phase 2, we plan to:
- Self-host the Vazirmatn font (no more Google Fonts)
- Cache thumbnails locally (no more Wikimedia requests for cached photos)
